How HealthPulse Works

From health data collection to early community awareness — converting complex epidemiological signals into plain-language public guidance.

Platform Processing Pipeline

The sequential intelligence pipeline powering HealthPulse surveillance.

01

DATA INGESTION

Continuous monitoring of incoming health signals, clinic visit tallies, and environmental factors across monitored sectors.

02

PREPROCESSING

Normalizing, cleaning, and deduplicating raw signals into standardized population density metrics per 100,000 residents.

03

BASELINE MONITORING

Calculating 4-week rolling historical averages to establish expected baseline health levels for each seasonal period.

04

ANOMALY DETECTION

Scanning for unusual statistical deviations (Z-score threshold shifts) that signal emerging disease clusters before major spikes occur.

05

RISK ASSESSMENT

Synthesizing multi-factor regional risk metrics (case growth, hospitalization ratios, population density) into simple risk categories.

06

EARLY WARNING

Triggering public alerts and regional notifications when disease activity surpasses safety threshold baselines.

07

PUBLIC INSIGHTS

Translating complex technical surveillance metrics into plain-language awareness, maps, and practical prevention steps for citizens.
